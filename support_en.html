❓ FAQ

How do I create a movie list?
Open the app, tap “New List” and add your favorite movies.

Can I share my lists with friends?
Yes! Go to your list and select “Share”.

I can't log in.
Please check your internet connection and ensure you have the latest version of the app. If the issue persists, contact us.

How do I create a room with friends?
Create a room from the social tab, invite your friends, and share your cinema discoveries together!

How do I delete my account?
Go to app settings, then "Account" and select "Delete my account". Your data will be deleted within 30 days.

Privacy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

No Tracking

LS CTRL is designed with privacy as a core feature. We believe your listening habits are your business, not ours.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Local Network

The app requires access to your local network solely to discover and communicate with your speakers (KEF LSX, LS50 Wireless, etc.).

  • No data leaves your network.
  • No cloud account is required.
  • No analytics or telemetry are included.

Third-Party Services

LS CTRL does not integrate with third-party tracking services or advertising networks. It is a direct interface between your device and your speaker.

Troubleshooting

Most issues with LS CTRL are related to network connectivity. Please ensure:

  • Your device is on the same Wi-Fi network as the speakers.
  • Local Network permissions are granted in iOS Settings.
  • Your speakers are powered on and not in deep standby.

Connection Issues

If the app cannot find your speakers, try entering the IP address manually if available, or restart both your router and the speakers.
